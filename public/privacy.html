Privacy Policy

Chachu's Sudoku · Last updated 18 November 2026

Chachu's Sudoku is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Android app, the iOS app or the website.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send any data to us or to third parties. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

The website only loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads. The mobile apps bundle everything they need and make no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your current puzzle, settings and game statistics on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) deletes it.

Permissions

The mobile app does not request any special permissions and does not use the internet.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/shibinnvc/sudoku/issues.
